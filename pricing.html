Frequently asked questions

What to know before scheduling

Use the estimate above to plan your service, then call us to confirm the vehicle and choose an available appointment time.

Is the displayed price guaranteed?

The displayed amount is an estimate based on your vehicle category, film, and selected service. We confirm the final price by phone after verifying the exact year, make, model, glass configuration, and whether old tint must be removed.

What is the difference between carbon and ceramic film?

Carbon film is a cost-effective option that improves privacy and comfort while rejecting approximately 50–60% of heat. Ceramic film is the premium option and is designed for stronger heat rejection up to 95% and 100% UV protection, while maintaining clearer visibility, and has a lifetime warranty.

Why can Tesla Model 3 pricing be different?

The Model 3 has an oversized rear windshield that can require additional film and installation time. You can still use the sedan estimate as a starting point, but please call us to confirm the exact price.

How much does old tint removal cost?

Tint removal starts at $25 as an add-on. The final amount depends on how many windows need removal and the condition of the existing film and adhesive.

Does the tint include a warranty?

Our professional tint installations include a lifetime warranty. We will explain the coverage and care instructions when your service is scheduled.

How do I reserve an appointment?

Tap “Call to Schedule With Us” or call (562) 220-8850. We will confirm the exact vehicle, service, price, expected installation time, and available appointment slots with you directly.
